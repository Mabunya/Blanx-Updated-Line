ABOUT

We started BLANX because we were tired of buying into brands that had nothing to say. Every piece here is something we'd actually wear: considered, not chased off a trend cycle. We work in small runs on purpose, for less waste, better quality control, and drops that still feel like drops when they land.

Every order is confirmed by a real person on WhatsApp before it ships, never a bot or an automated email. Need help with fit or colour? Message us and we'll talk it through. That's the whole philosophy: fewer things, done properly, by people who'll actually talk to you.
